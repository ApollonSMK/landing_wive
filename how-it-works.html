Guide

How it Works

From connecting your wallet to collecting your winnings — everything you need to know.

01

Connect with World App

Open WIVE inside the World App. Your World ID is your identity — no MetaMask, no browser extensions, no seed phrases. Just open and go.

  • Open World App on your phone
  • Navigate to WIVE mini-app
  • You're verified automatically via World ID
02

Choose & Predict

Pick one of three assets — Bitcoin, Ethereum, or Worldcoin. Each asset has an active round running. You decide: will the price go UP or DOWN in the next 5 minutes?

  • Select BTC, ETH, or WLD
  • See the current price from Binance in real-time
  • Choose UP (green) or DOWN (red)
  • Set your bet amount in WLD
03

Wait 5 Minutes

The round runs for 5 minutes. Watch the live price chart as the countdown ticks. When time's up, the round closes automatically.

  • Watch the live sparkline chart
  • See total pool size and bet distribution
  • Countdown timer shows remaining time
04

Collect Your Winnings

If you predicted correctly, you win a proportional share of the losing side's pool. Settlements happen on-chain automatically — no one needs to approve your payout.

  • Correct predictions split the losing pool
  • Claim your WLD rewards directly
  • View your history in the Results page
  • Track your win rate on the Leaderboard

About

What is WIVE?

The first decentralized prediction market on World Chain, where verified humans compete to predict crypto prices.

Our Mission

WIVE was built with one goal: create a fair, transparent prediction market accessible to everyone. By combining World Chain's speed with World ID's human verification, we've eliminated the biggest problems in prediction markets — bots, manipulation, and opaque settlement.

100% On-Chain

Every bet, every settlement, every payout — everything happens on World Chain. Our smart contract is verified and open-source. Your funds are never held by us. No custodians, no trust required.

Human Only

Every user on WIVE is verified through World ID. This means every player is a real, unique human. No bots, no multi-accounts, no unfair advantages. Just pure skill and prediction ability.

Fast & Automated

Rounds last 5 minutes. Our settler bot runs 24/7, automatically settling finished rounds and creating new ones. No delays, no downtime, no manual intervention. The market never sleeps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions.

WIVE is a decentralized prediction market built on World Chain. You predict whether the price of BTC, ETH, or WLD will go up or down in the next 5 minutes. If you're right, you earn from the losing pool. Everything is on-chain, transparent, and settled automatically.

WIVE is a Mini App inside the World App. To use it:

  1. Download and open the World App on your phone
  2. Navigate to the Mini Apps section
  3. Search for WIVE and open it

You cannot access the prediction market from a regular web browser — World ID verification is required.

You need:

  • World App — with a verified World ID
  • WLD tokens — in your World App wallet to place bets

That's it. No MetaMask, no seed phrases, no browser extensions.

Each prediction round lasts 5 minutes. When a round ends, it is automatically settled by our on-chain bot, and a new round begins immediately. The market runs 24/7 with zero downtime.

Prices are fetched in real-time from the Binance API. The price at the start of a round is the "lock price", and the price at the end is the "close price". If the close price is higher than the lock price, UP wins. If lower, DOWN wins.

When a round is settled:

  • The winning side splits the losing side's pool proportionally
  • A small protocol fee (3%) is deducted from the total pool
  • Your winnings are available to claim directly on-chain

Example: If you bet 1 WLD on UP and UP wins, and the UP pool is 10 WLD while the DOWN pool is 15 WLD, your share would be 1/10 × 15 × 0.97 = ~1.455 WLD.

WIVE is fully on-chain:

  • Smart contract is verified and open-source on WorldScan
  • Your funds are never held by us — they go directly to the smart contract
  • Settlement is automated and cannot be tampered with
  • All users are verified humans via World ID — no bots

Troubleshooting

Having issues? Try these solutions.

App won't load

Make sure you're opening WIVE inside the World App, not in a regular browser. WIVE requires World ID verification and will not work outside the World App.

Transaction failed

Check that you have enough WLD in your wallet to cover the bet amount plus gas fees. Also ensure you're connected to the World Chain network.

Round didn't settle

Rounds are settled by our automated bot. In rare cases, there may be a brief delay. Settlements typically complete within 30 seconds of a round ending. If a round remains unsettled for more than 5 minutes, contact us.

Profile not showing

Your profile is created automatically on your first visit. If your avatar or stats aren't loading, try closing and reopening the mini app. Your on-chain data is always safe.

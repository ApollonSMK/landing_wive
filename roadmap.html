Roadmap

  • Sybil-resistant onboarding via World ID proof-of-personhood

  • Autonomous settler bot running 24/7 — zero downtime operations
  • On-chain leaderboard with win rate & PnL tracking
  • Dynamic avatar & identity system (NFT-ready)
  • Mini App integration with World App ecosystem

Phase 3 — Tokenomics

$WIVE Token Launch

The native utility token powering the entire WIVE protocol.

Token Utility

  • $WIVE as native currency for prediction markets
  • Fee discounts for $WIVE-denominated predictions
  • Governance voting rights — propose & vote on new markets
  • Staking rewards — earn yield by securing the protocol
  • Premium features gated by $WIVE holding tiers

Token Distribution

  • Community & Ecosystem — 40% (airdrops, rewards, incentives)
  • Protocol Treasury — 20% (development, operations, grants)
  • Team & Advisors — 15% (4-year vesting, 1-year cliff)
  • Liquidity Provision — 10% (DEX pools, market making)
  • Early Contributors & OGs — 10% (retroactive airdrop)
  • Strategic Partners — 5% (integrations, collaborations)

Launch Strategy

  • Fair launch — no VC rounds, no pre-sale dumps
  • Initial DEX Offering (IDO) on World Chain DEXs
  • Liquidity locked for 12 months minimum
  • Anti-bot & anti-snipe launch protections
Phase 4 — DeFi Layer

Staking & Yield Protocol

Transforming WIVE from a prediction market into a yield-generating protocol.

  • Single-sided $WIVE staking vault — earn protocol fees
  • LP staking — provide liquidity, earn boosted $WIVE rewards
  • Tiered staking — Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond with escalating APY
  • veWIVE (vote-escrowed) — lock tokens for governance power & boosted yield
  • Revenue sharing — % of all prediction fees distributed to stakers
  • Auto-compounding vaults via smart contract
  • Prediction pools — stake and let strategies trade for you
Phase 5 — Governance

DAO & Decentralization

Handing the protocol to the community — fully decentralized governance.

  • WIVE DAO launch — on-chain governance via Snapshot + Tally
  • Governance proposals — add/remove markets, adjust fees, allocate treasury
  • Community grants program — fund builders, integrations, content
  • Multi-sig treasury managed by elected council
  • Protocol upgrades via timelock + governance vote
  • Bug bounty DAO — community-managed security program
  • Full contract ownership transferred to DAO
Phase 6 — Multi-Chain

Cross-Chain Expansion

Taking WIVE beyond World Chain — everywhere DeFi users live.

  • Deployment on Base, Arbitrum, Optimism (EVM chains)
  • Cross-chain bridging via LayerZero / Wormhole
  • Unified liquidity across all chains
  • Chain-specific prediction markets (L2 gas tokens, etc.)
  • Solana deployment for sub-second settlement
